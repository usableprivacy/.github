## About Usable Privacy

**Usable Privacy** develops open-source tools and services that make
privacy technology practical and accessible. Our tools block online ads to help protect your online privacy.

## Projects

### 🏡 Usable Privacy Box

The Usable Privacy Box is a privacy-focused solution for home and small networks.

It protects computers, smartphones, smart TVs and other connected devices
at the network level by filtering advertising and tracking services.

**→ [Box Documentation](https://docs.usableprivacy.com/box)**  
**→ [Source-Code](https://github.com/usableprivacy/config)**  

**→ [UP4 Box Bestellen](https://nili.cc/de/box/)**

#### upribox

The UP Box grew out of upribox, the first Usable Privacy project, which
received funding from netidee. Visit the [original website](https://upribox.org)
or explore the [(archived) upribox GitHub project](https://github.com/usableprivacy/upribox).

### 🌐 Usable Privacy Service

The **Usable Privacy DNS Service** is a public DNS service designed to
filter advertising, trackers and malicious domains.

It supports encrypted DNS protocols to protect DNS requests while they
are transmitted over the internet.

> https://adfree.usableprivacy.net/dns-query

**→ [DNS documentation](https://docs.usableprivacy.com/dns)**  
**→ [Source code](https://github.com/usableprivacy/updns)**

As of 2025 an invite-only service is also available at [nili.cc/service](https://nili.cc/service/).
